Hi, I'm Vivian Oh.

I started taking photos on my mother's old digital camera during a family beach trip and never really put it down since then. Most of what I shoot happens outdoors — coastlines, backseats, the ten minutes of vibrant light before the sun sets.

A lot of my favorite frames happened by accident: a windshield catching the sunrise, a bouquet backlit by accident, a wave that broke right as I pressed the shutter. A large source of my inspiration are the people around me, including my younger sister, a frequent subject of my lens.

Lately I've been thinking about how photography and fashion overlap: the way light shapes fabric, the way a silhouette can say more than a face. I'd love to bring that same eye — patient, a little imperfect, capturing the light — to photographing people and clothes in motion.
